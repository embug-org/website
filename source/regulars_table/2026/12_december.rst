December 2026
=============

The next regulars table will take place on December 2nd, 2026, at 6:00 p.m at Realraum Graz.

This time, we won't be meeting at a restaurant, but at the "Realraum" hackerspace.
We want to pay a visit to the organization and tour the Realraum facilities.
Among other things, there's an amateur radio club station, a biolab, workshops, and equipment available to members.
After the tour, we'd like to sit together in the common room and order some pizza.

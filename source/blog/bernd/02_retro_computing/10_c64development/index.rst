C64 Development
===============

The C64 was one of the best-selling computer systems in the world.
At the same time, the CPU, graphics card and sound chip are very, very simple.
That doesn’t mean the C64 isn’t a complex system. No, not at all...

But it's a system where a single person can still fully understand the ENTIRE
system. Really everything. Starting with the "operating system", called KERNAL,
all the way to sound and graphics generation.

The system is still so popular today that new games are still being developed
for it.

I also enjoy programming for this machine.
That doesn't mean I'm writing hex values into memory areas on the C64 itself.
No. I do use a modern PC with a modern editor (some might disagree with me here
- my editor of choice is VI) and I definitely wouldn't want to do without GIT.

On the following pages, I'd like to introduce you to my development environment.
Maybe one or two of you will be inspired to get into retro development as well.
